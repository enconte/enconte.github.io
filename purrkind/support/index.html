PurrKind: Cat Rescue Support

Help and contact

Found a bug, have an idea or need help with a purchase? Email us at purrkind.soporte@gmail.com. We answer in English or Spanish, usually within a few days.

To help you faster, tell us your phone model, your Android or iOS version, the game version (shown on the store page) and, if possible, a screenshot.

The game is currently available in Spanish only.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. It saves automatically every 30 seconds and when you leave the game. There are no accounts or cloud saves: if you uninstall the game or switch phones, your progress cannot be recovered.

I bought the Premium Pack and I don't see it

On iPhone and iPad, go to Extras > Restaurar compras using the same Apple account you bought it with. On Android, purchases come back automatically when you open the game with the same Google account; if it doesn't appear, fully close the game and open it again while online. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the store receipt.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Google Play and Apple, so refunds are requested directly from them: Google Play or Apple.

The "watch ad" button doesn't show up

Ads are always optional, and sometimes none is available (it depends on your connection and region). Wait a few minutes and try again.

Why didn't I earn anything while I was away?

Your cats collect purrs up to a time limit (2 hours at first; some cats extend it). If you set your device's clock back, the game gives no earnings for that period.

What happens to my cats if I put them up for adoption?

You start over with Michi, but you receive Huellitas (paw prints) that boost production forever. Huellitas, the album and the Premium Pack are kept.

How do I mute the game?

In Extras > Sonido. Music you're playing on your phone keeps playing.

Privacy and your data

You can delete your statistics in Extras > Mis estadísticas. Everything else is explained in the privacy policy.
