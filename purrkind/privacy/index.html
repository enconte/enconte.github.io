PurrKind: Cat Rescue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · The Spanish version prevails in case of discrepancy.

This policy explains what data is processed when you play PurrKind: Cat Rescue (“the game”) on Android and iOS, what it is used for, and what you can do about it. The game has no user accounts: we never ask for your name, email or any other information to play.

1. Controller

Eduardo Conte (“enconte”), an independent developer based in Argentina.
Contact: purrkind.soporte@gmail.com

2. What data is processed and why

Your saved game (on your device only)

Your progress (cats, levels, purrs, upgrades, active purchases and preferences such as sound) is stored on your device. It is not sent to any server and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a reward. We use Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (AAID on Android; IDFA on iOS, only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt),
  • your IP address (from which an approximate location is inferred),
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads.

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, cap their frequency and prevent fraud. Personalized ads are only shown if you consent when asked (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) or if you have not opted out through your device settings. Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services and read Google's privacy policy.

Usage statistics (Unity Analytics)

To understand how the game is played and improve it, we use Unity Analytics by Unity Technologies. It receives:

  • a random installation identifier generated by Unity (not the advertising identifier),
  • gameplay events: session start, tutorial progress, level-ups, rescues, upgrades bought, areas unlocked, Adoptions, ad offers shown and watched, and purchases made,
  • technical data: device model and operating system, language, game version and approximate country or region based on the IP address.

This data is not used for advertising and is not combined with AdMob data. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

In-game purchases

Purchases (for example, the Premium Pack) are processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store. We only receive the purchase confirmation and which product was bought; we never see your payment details. The privacy policies of Google and Apple apply.

3. Legal bases

We process data to provide the game and any purchases you make, based on our legitimate interest in keeping it running, funding it with optional ads and improving it, and, where required by law (for example, in Europe for personalized ads and statistics), on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Who we share data with

Only with the providers listed above: Google (AdMob and Google Play), Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics) and Apple (App Store), who act under their own policies. We do not sell your data. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, with the safeguards described in their policies.

5. Retention

Your saved game stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Statistics and advertising data are kept by Unity and Google for the periods set in their policies, or until you ask for deletion.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: always optional; if you don't tap “Watch ad”, none is shown.
  • Consent (Europe, UK and Switzerland): in the game, Extras > Privacidad > Opciones lets you change your choices.
  • Tracking on iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads), where you can delete or reset it.
  • Delete your statistics: in the game, Extras > Mis estadísticas > Borrar. This asks Unity to erase everything collected from your installation, and the game stops sending statistics from that device.
  • Delete your saved game: uninstall the game.

You can also request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to purrkind.soporte@gmail.com. Since there are no accounts, we may need you to use the in-game option so we can locate your data.

Residents of Argentina may exercise their rights under Law No. 25,326 and file complaints with the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent to Google and Unity encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfect, but we try to send only what is necessary.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with its date. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
